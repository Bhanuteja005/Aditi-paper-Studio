# Aditi Paperie — image prompts for ChatGPT

**How to use**
1. Open ChatGPT (image generation) and paste **Step 1 — the brief** first. It sets the style for the whole shoot.
2. Then paste the prompts one at a time. Ask for the size listed.
3. Save each image with the exact **file name** shown, as `.jpg`, into
   `public/img/` (in the project root) — the site picks it up automatically (no code change), replacing the Unsplash photo.
4. If a result shows text, logos, plastic or odd hands → ask ChatGPT to "regenerate without …".

---

## Step 1 — paste this brief first

> I'm creating a photo set for **Aditi Paperie**, a handmade paper studio at **Kanha Shanti Vanam** near Hyderabad, India
> (a calm, green meditation campus). They make **100% tree-free, chemical-free, archival paper by hand** from
> **textile-mill cotton rags, banana stem, coconut fibre, water hyacinth and fallen leaves**. The paper is made by
> **local Indian village women** in a simple, airy studio with stone vats, wooden moulds and deckles, a rag beater,
> drying racks and sunlight. Products: loose sheets with deckled edges, hand-bound diaries, letter sets, wedding
> invitations, archival folios for photographs, paper lampshades, frames.
>
> **Style for every image (keep it consistent):** clean, calm, premium editorial photography like a luxury Indian craft
> brand (think Jaipur Rugs / Nicobar). Soft natural daylight from a window or open sky, gentle shadows. Muted, warm
> palette that matches the brand: **off-white paper (#FAF8F3), warm beige (#F2EDE3), soft charcoal/ink (#1A1916) and
> mustard gold (#C8962E)** — use gold as a small accent only (a sari border, thread, a brass object, a dried leaf).
> Lots of negative space, uncluttered backgrounds, real textures (cotton fibre, deckle edges, stone, wood, linen).
> Shot on a full-frame camera, 50mm or 85mm, shallow depth of field, photorealistic, natural skin and hands.
>
> **Never include:** text, letters, logos, watermarks, brand names, plastic, neon colours, busy backgrounds,
> extra fingers or distorted hands, Western models, stock-photo smiles.
>
> Reply "ready" and wait for my prompts.

Short **style suffix** (add to the end of any prompt if the look drifts):
`— calm editorial craft photography, soft natural daylight, off-white, beige, charcoal and mustard-gold palette, lots of negative space, photorealistic, no text, no logos.`

## Best practice
- Generate **hero + banner images first** — they make the biggest difference.
- Real photos of Aditi, the women and the actual studio will always beat generated ones — use them for
  `about-founder`, `women-banner`, `about-women` as soon as they exist.
- Keep people's clothing in muted tones (ivory, beige, olive, mustard) so every page feels like one family.
